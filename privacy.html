Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains what information Snake Quest ("the site") collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address or other personal details through the game. Your game settings, best scores and sound preference are stored only in your own browser (local storage). You can clear them at any time from your browser settings.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Hosting and analytics

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like most hosts, Vercel may process technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver the site securely. We use Vercel Web Analytics to collect anonymous page view data to understand how visitors use the site. This analytics service does not use cookies and does not track users across sites. The data collected includes page URLs, referrer information, browser type, and general geographic location (country/region).

Children

The site is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
