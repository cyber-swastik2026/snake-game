About Snake Quest

Snake Quest is a free, colorful version of the classic Snake game that runs right in your browser. There is nothing to download or install, and it works on computers, tablets and phones.

What you can play

Pick from 16 game modes: Classic, Walls, No borders, Portal, Poison, Statue, Twin, Peaceful, Key, Sokoban, Light, Minesweeper, Magnet, Cheese, Shield and Arrow. Each mode changes the rules, so the game stays fresh.

Make it yours

Open the settings (⚙) to choose your fruit, number of apples, speed, board size, snake color and board theme. Your settings and best scores are saved in your own browser.

Controls

  • Move: arrow keys, W A S D, swipe, or the on-screen D-pad (🎮 button)
  • Play / replay: SPACE
  • Pause / resume: P or Esc
